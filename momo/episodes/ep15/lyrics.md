# 야옹 멍멍 짝짝

Palette: Korean toddler clap chant with plucky synth and woodblock

사용자 한국어 짝곡 요청. 학습 단어는 고양이·강아지 두 개, 의성어와 박수로 짧은 후렴을 반복한다. 아래는 생성 요청 가사이며 실제 가창은 결과에서 확인한다.

```style
Bright clear light youthful female solo, Korean toddler clap-chant dance song for ages 2-4. 120 BPM, steady bouncy 4/4 beat, dry handclaps on 2 and 4, soft kick, round plucky synth bass, playful woodblock and tiny tom accents. Native Korean pronunciation, clearly separate every syllable of go-yang-i and gang-a-ji. One short three-note hook, repeat the exact melody and rhythm every time. Brief animal-name call then easy meow or woof response, clear small breaths. Friendly pitched singing, not spoken rap. Light sparse accompaniment below the lead; tiny unison doubles only on responses. Begin singing within one second, no instrumental break, no long build. Lively and catchy without rushing. Natural short sung ending and final chord; no restart or long outro. About 75 seconds.
```
