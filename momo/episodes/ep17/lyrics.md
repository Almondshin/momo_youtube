Style: Clear bright youthful female solo, very clear Korean vowels and final consonants, childlike light voice with natural pitch. Original rhythmic toddler nursery song, 120 BPM, cute toybox bubblegum pop, marimba ostinato and handclaps with simple warm bass. ONE short memorable repeated melodic hook, simple and bouncy, short-short-long accents. Main words 공 and 자동차, sound hooks 통통 and 빵빵. Carefully finish every sung word. No melisma, no belting. Every line on steady 4-beat bar, small pauses only inside phrases for toddlers to echo. Sing continuously through all four sections, no instrumental bridge or solo, no extra chorus or adlibs. Start singing in first second, immediate next verse after chorus, final 야호 fully pronounced then 2-second resolved ending. About 68 seconds total.
