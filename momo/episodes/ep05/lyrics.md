# ep05 "Rainbow Colors" (무지개 색깔) — lyrics v1 (2026-10-01)

Status: draft v1. The topic and these lyrics still need the user's [승인] before the song is made.

Direction: the house format since ep04. English lyrics on a Korean children's-song (동요) rhythm, for Korean families
whose toddlers (2–4) learn English (user direction 2026-09-30). Topic: the six rainbow colours in order: red, orange,
yellow, green, blue, purple. Each colour gets one clear still object. Momo looks for it, points, sings, and does one
safe action per colour. The chorus names the colours in order. Written to the ep03/ep04 lessons (PRODUCTION.md (9)):
- a 4-bar instrumental intro leaves room for the library greeting,
- chorus lines are 1 bar long and pair into shots of about 4 s,
- each verse line is one picture, and objects stay still,
- Momo only claps, sways, nods, waves, points or bounces gently; Ducky does the hops,
- the backing answers `( )` come after the lead line with a gap between them. This helps the aligner time the captions.

Story: a rainbow is up in the sky, and Momo finds each of its colours in the garden. Scenes (runbook 4단계):
① intro + chorus 1 ② red, orange + chorus 2 ③ yellow, green + chorus 3 ④ blue, purple (Ducky) ⑤ quiz bridge + final
chorus + bye. Each colour word is sung 10–11 times: 4 in the choruses, 5–6 in its own verse and 1 in the bridge.

Open choice for the user: Korean kids learn a 7-colour rainbow (빨주노초파남보). These lyrics use 6 colours, because
"indigo" is not a toddler word. The rainbow picture must also show exactly 6 stripes so it matches the words.

Target: about 124 BPM (Suno tends to land around 127) and 2:10–2:20 in total. The lyrics have 45 lines and 168 words;
ep04 had 51 lines and 238 words and came out at 2:20. Counting from ep04's real section lengths, plus the 4-bar
intro, gives about 2:15. The cuts are laid out on the real song after import, so nothing below is fixed yet.

## Shots

Set: the ep04 garden ("a sunny green garden with soft grass, a small white picket fence and a bright blue sky"). In the
chorus shots, a big rainbow arcs across the sky. Every object close-up sits on "a plain cream-white picnic blanket on
the soft green grass, the sunny garden with the white picket fence softly blurred behind". The blanket must be plain,
with no check pattern, so each colour stands alone and the backgrounds match.

A shot where Momo is visible is a wan2_7 lip-sync shot: frontal start image, both feet on the ground, both long ears
fully visible, and the action written into the prompt. Object and rainbow shots are seedance motion clips: the object
stays completely still, and the motion comes from tiny sparkles that twinkle and a soft breeze in the grass.
