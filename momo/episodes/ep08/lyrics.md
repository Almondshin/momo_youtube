# ep08 "Shapes" (도형) — lyrics v1.1 (2026-10-01, reviewed)

User direction (ep04, kept for every song): English lyrics on a Korean children's-song (동요) rhythm, for Korean
families whose toddlers (2–4) learn English. Topic: finding shapes around us — circle (the sun), square (a window),
triangle (a slice of watermelon), star (a star in the sky), heart (a heart-shaped cookie). The chorus names the five
shapes. Each verse block is the same little game: Momo looks at a thing, asks "What shape is it?", everyone answers
and Momo traces the shape in the air with one paw. Ducky gets the big move: it hops around in a circle. Draft for the
user's approval. The user makes the song in Suno Pro (settings at the end). The take sets the real timing, and the
cut table is laid on its bar grid (PRODUCTION.md (9)).

Title: "Shapes Song with Momo the Bunny | Circle, Square, Triangle for Toddlers".

Plan at ~124 BPM (one 4/4 bar ≈ 1.94 s; Suno usually lands at 125–127). The song has 48 short lines and about 200
words. That is close to ep06: its 192 words were sung by 1:41, and Suno then added a 31 s instrumental outro, which the
video cut at 1:45. The take itself ran 2:14 because the length was set to 2:15. ep06's real take sang each 6-line
chorus in exactly 6 bars. There is no bridge, which keeps the song near 1:50 and leaves no gap where Suno could put
an instrumental break.
