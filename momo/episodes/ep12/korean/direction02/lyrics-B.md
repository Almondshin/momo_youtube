# 사과 톡 바나나 통 | 모모타임 주고받기 시안

## Style

```style
Original Korean toddler call-and-response chant-song for ages 2-4, 116 BPM, steady 4/4 boom-clap pulse with playful small tom and woodblock accents, simple bouncing synth notes, sparse clean accompaniment. Very bright light youthful girl-like female lead; a small childlike unison group echoes only the parenthesized fruit names. All phrases are pitched and sung, including the responses. A memorable four-note hook with separated short syllables and tiny echo gaps; apple and banana calls land strongly on the beat. Precise native Korean pronunciation, comfortable narrow pitch range, lively rhythmic repetition, clear main voice above the beat. Sing from the first second; keep the pulse through the whole song, no long buildup. Repeat the same hook without melodic embellishment and land a short complete final chord. Compact 40-50 second hook audition.
```
