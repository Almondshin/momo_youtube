# Cat! Dog! Meow! Woof!

Palette: rhythmic preschool animal dance

```style
Bright light female lead, playful preschool nursery dance song for ages 2-4. Crisp rhythmic call-and-response hook. 118 BPM, springy bass, short marimba plucks, dry handclaps, light kick and shaker. Very clear cat and dog words, tiny two- or three-note melodic phrases, short syllables landing on the beat, easy cute meow and woof responses. Catchy child-friendly bounce, minimal intro under one second, continuous singing with no instrumental break. Warm small vocal doubles only on echoes. Finish the final tag and resolve naturally; no vocal restart or long outro.
```

```
[Hook - immediate sung hook, playful bouncy rhythm]
Cat, cat! Meow, meow, meow!
Dog, dog! Woof, woof, woof!
Cat! Meow! Dog! Woof!
Meow, meow! Woof, woof!

[Verse - crisp short syllables, space for little echoes]
Little cat! Meow, meow!
Little dog! Woof, woof!
Cat goes meow! Meow, meow!
Dog goes woof! Woof, woof!

[Hook - same memorable tune]
Cat, cat! Meow, meow, meow!
Dog, dog! Woof, woof, woof!
Cat! Meow! Dog! Woof!
Meow, meow! Woof, woof!

[Action - keep singing over the beat]
Clap, clap! Meow, meow!
Clap, clap! Woof, woof!
Meow, meow! Woof, woof!
Clap, clap, clap!

[Final hook - one clear ending, no restart]
Cat, cat! Meow, meow, meow!
Dog, dog! Woof, woof, woof!
Cat! Meow! Dog! Woof!
Meow, meow! Woof, woof!

[Outro - short sung tag, resolved final chord]
Cat! Dog!
Meow! Woof!
[End]
```
