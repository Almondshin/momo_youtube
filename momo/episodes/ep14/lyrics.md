# Cat / Dog — 후렴·리듬 수정 v2

사용자: A 80%, 리듬·후렴을 더 신나고 중독성 있게 + 따라하기 쉽게.

기존 A를 커버의 실제 음원 참조로 사용한다. 기존 여성 보컬과 기본 분위기를 유지하도록 요청하되, 결과가 그대로 유지되는지는 반환 음원에서 확인한다. 핵심 패턴은 Cat-cat / Meow-meow, Dog-dog / Woof-woof. `little/goes` 문장을 빼고 같은 리듬·짧은 음형을 반복한다. 기존 원곡과 가사는 보존한다.

```style
Preserve the source recording's bright light feminine lead voice and warm preschool tone. Refine its rhythmic hook rather than changing genre. Around 118 BPM, bouncy simple nursery dance for ages 2-4. One stable two-note singalong motif, exactly repeated. Cat-cat then meow-meow; dog-dog then woof-woof. Four clear syllable pulses in each bar, short breaths between calls and responses, never rushed. Punchy dry handclaps on beats 2 and 4, round springy bass, short bright marimba plucks, light kick and shaker. More infectious rhythmic bounce and a memorable repeat, sparse backing so every word is clear. Lead sings the words; small soft echo doubles only. Vocal within one second. Continuous sung sections, no instrumental break. Finish the last sung tag naturally and stop within about 70-75 seconds, short final chord, no long outro.
```
